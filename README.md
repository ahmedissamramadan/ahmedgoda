# Ahmed Gouda — Interior Design & Renovation Portfolio

## Overview
This repository contains the source code for the professional portfolio of **Ahmed Gouda**, showcasing his expertise in interior design, home renovation, and space transformation.

## Project Details
- **Role**: Front-end Development & UI/UX Implementation
- **Tech Stack**: React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui
- **Live Demo**: [View Live Project](https://ahmedgoda.lovable.app/) *(Link will be updated to final domain)*

## Key Features
- **Visual-First Design**: Focuses heavily on high-quality imagery to showcase design work.
- **Before/After Comparisons**: Interactive or side-by-side displays of renovation projects (bathrooms, bedrooms, dining areas, hallways, living rooms).
- **Service Breakdown**: Clear presentation of design and renovation services offered.
- **Trust Elements**: Integration of TrustBadges, TestimonialCards, and a clear ProcessStep workflow.
- **Lead Generation**: Integrated ContactForm for prospective clients.

## What I Accomplished
I developed a visually striking portfolio that serves as a digital showroom for Ahmed Gouda's interior design projects. By highlighting the dramatic before-and-after transformations and outlining a clear service process, the site effectively builds trust and encourages potential clients to initiate contact for their renovation needs.
